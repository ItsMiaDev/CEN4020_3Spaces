Third-Space Finder
Project Overview
Third-Space Finder is a software application designed to help people find places outside of their home, school, or workplace where they can spend time, socialize, relax, or participate in activities.

The application focuses on helping users find a place based on the type of experience they are looking for, rather than simply searching for businesses by name or location.

Problem:
People often want to get out of the house but may not know where to go. Existing map and business-review applications provide information about businesses and locations, but they do not necessarily focus on characteristics such as social atmosphere, noise level, whether a place is comfortable for someone visiting alone, or how suitable a location is for spending several hours.
Third-Space Finder aims to make it easier for users to discover spaces based on the experience they are looking for.

Target Users:
The initial target users are young adults and college students who are looking for places to socialize, relax, participate in activities, or spend time outside of home, school, and work.
The application is not limited to college students and could eventually be used by anyone looking for a third space.

Initial Features:
The initial version of the application is expected to include:
Searching for nearby third spaces
Filtering locations based on user preferences
Viewing information about individual locations
Viewing locations on a map
Searching using natural-language descriptions
Receiving recommendations based on user preferences
Viewing information about characteristics such as cost, noise level, and social atmosphere
Allowing users to contribute reviews and ratings about their experiences
